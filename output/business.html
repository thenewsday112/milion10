Taylor Swift Skips Kansas City Chiefs' Game Against Denver Broncos

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Taylor Swift apparently decided to turn down the opportunity to get a little Mile High as the Kansas City Chiefs faced off against the Denver Broncos. 

The pop star, 36, did not appear to be in attendance for the Chiefs’ Sunday, November 1, AFC West showdown against the Broncos at Empower Field. 

Swift’s apparent absence continued her recent trend of not traveling with husband Travis Kelce and the team for road games. Instead, the pop star appeared to spend the day at a FYC screening of Toy Story 5 alongside the beloved animated film’s director Andrew Stanton. 

“Yeah, so I was on a tour that was, um, a lot of things but it was pretty physically exhausting and it was a 3-and-1/2 hour show and one of those tours — I have actually never experienced this level of exhaustion between shows,” Swift recalled of her history-making Eras Tour and how her brother, Austin Swift, convinced her to lend her voice to the film’s soundtrack. “I was like in bed all the days that I wasn’t playing a show just trying to rest up.”

Former Chiefs Star Mitchell Schwartz Trashes Team for Signing Justin Tucker

She continued, via social media footage, “So my brother contacts me … sometimes we talk like siblings and sometime we have business conversations that are half-business, half-siblings … he calls me up and he’s like, ‘Hey, I really think you need to see Toy Story 5.'”

Safe to say, Swift’s calendar remains full as her husband experiences what is said to be his last season in the NFL.

Earlier this week, Kelce, 37, responded after Prince William jokingly said he tried bumping the Chiefs star out of a selfie he took with Swift at an Eras Tour stop in England in 2024. 

“I get it, man,” Kelce said on the Wednesday, October 28, episode of his “New Heights” podcast. “I was just a boyfriend at that point, you know? I wasn’t her husband yet, you know.”

Kelce joked that the Prince, 44, was likely thinking, “’We don’t really know if this is going to last, so [can you] just move out of the way real quick so we can keep this photo forever?’”

Swift and Kelce got married in July at New York City’s Madison Square Garden.

Since tying the knot, Swift has appeared at every Chiefs home game at Arrowhead Stadium. She last attended Kansas City’s 22-6 win over the Los Angeles Chargers on October 18. At that game, beauty influencer Ashlie Molstad described the scene when Swift took her throne inside her suite during the first quarter. 

“Three big ass dudes come in and they are there for security for Taylor,” Moldstad explained. “Then you just kind of start observing what the f*** is going on and what that world is like. [The security team] is head on a swivel as if they’re Secret Service, right? I mean, she is the most famous, at least American, celebrity that we have right.”

She continued, “Anyway, there’s these three men and they’re very friendly to us. They’re very nice. But they are very locked into…they are not watching the game. They’re watching people. It was kind of wild to see how many people are constantly looking.”

The Chiefs return home for their next game, hosting the New York Jets on Sunday, October 8.

Martha Stewart Breaks Silence After Halloween Costume Backlash

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Martha Stewart is breaking her silence after sparking controversy with her “hungry” Halloween 2026 costume.

“I am distressed that my Halloween costume yesterday, which was intended to bring more awareness to the very serious subject of homelessness, has offended people,” Stewart, 85, told Page Six in a Sunday, November 1, statement. “[That] was never my intention.”

Hours earlier, Stewart revealed her annual costume via social media. In the since-deleted snapshot screenshotted by Page Six, Stewart wore tattered clothes with what appeared to be dirt — or makeup made to simulate dirt — smeared across her face. She also held up a sign that read, “I AM HUNGRY. HELP ME.”

Stewart quickly deleted the photo amid instant backlash for allegedly mocking individuals experiencing homelessness.

“Homelessness continues to remain one of America’s greatest challenges and ongoing crises, something that I care deeply about and I have spent a significant amount of time and effort supporting charities that combat this ongoing social epidemic,” she stressed in her Sunday statement. “[I am] committed to continuing to make a difference.”

Stewart has not further addressed the controversy. Us reached out for comment.

The lifestyle mogul has long been candid about the importance of giving back to communities in need.

Martha Stewart Details What She's Learned by Decade

“My life has touched so many people’s lives in this room, and I really am very grateful for getting an award like this,” she said at 2025 amFAR Palm Beach, accepting the accolade for Award of Inspiration. “In preparing for tonight, I reflected on the woman who has brought us together [late amFAR founder], Dr. Matilda Crim. She knew what I know that in philanthropy and in business to accomplish anything you have to begin with seeing a need. What can you do to help fill that void? I’m always looking to fill a void, and she certainly did.”

At the time, Stewart noted her support of amFAR — which funds AIDs research — through the years.

“In October 1985, a month after Dr. Crim co-founded amFAR, I opened my home to a very dear friend so he could die surrounded by love and comfort,” she recalled. “He was married to a woman I was very close to, but he also had relationships with men. My friend and his wife had two little children, 5 years old and just over 1 year old, and before he died, I promised him that I would show his children the world. I did. In 1985, that was all we had to give. Comfort and our word.”

Chad Lowe Lists $3.2M Home After 13-Year-Old Daughter Fiona's Death

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Business  |  Read Source →

Chad Lowe and his wife, Kim Painter, have put their Los Angeles home on the market after the untimely death of their 13-year-old daughter, Fiona.

Lowe, 58, and Painter, 52, recently put their four-bedroom house up for sale, according to real estate records viewed by Us on Saturday, October 31. The couple accepted an offer for $3.249 million, contingent on certain conditions being met. (TMZ was first to report the news.)

Lowe and Painter, who purchased the property in March 2025 months after losing their house in the devastating Palisades wildfires, have not publicly addressed the real estate move. Us has reached out for comment.

One month earlier, Lowe and Painter announced that their 13-year-old daughter had died by suicide. (They also share daughters Mabel, 17, and Nixie, 10.)

Chad Lowe's Daughter Fiona Dead at 13: 'She Was the Light of Our Lives'

“We are deeply heartbroken by the loss of our beloved Fiona,” Lowe and Painter wrote in a September statement, remembering Fiona as “a loving young girl who was smart, creative and loved to dance.”

They continued at the time, “She was adored by her sisters and she was deeply loved by her parents. In fact, she was the light of our lives. Her passing is devastating for our entire family and we ask for prayers and privacy at this time. If you or someone you know is struggling with mental health, please reach out to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline by calling or texting 988.”

Fiona’s older sister, Mabel, has also been candid about grieving the death of her sibling.

“I love you so much, Fiona ❤️. I wish your short time here was easier and I hope your mind is at ease now,” she wrote via Instagram on October 3. “I will honor you forever and keep your memory alive. I love you, I hope you knew just how much.”

Lowe, for his part, returned to social media days later to repost a poignant message about grief and healing.

“We both possess the human and the divine,” Gerry Hussey said during a 2025 appearance on the “Of Healing” podcast, which Lowe shared via his Instagram Stories on October 8. “Through consciousness and awareness when we awaken the peaceful observer and we get beyond our physical human emotions and our physical human needs and the needs of the ego, and we just let go and we release all that and we connect to a feeling, that is the divine frequency.”

Two days later, the Pretty Little Liars alum and Painter made a joint appearance with Mabel and Nixie at nephew Matthew Lowe’s wedding to Emily Markus.
