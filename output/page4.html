'SNL' Pays Tribute to Patrick Stewart Following Late Actor's Death

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

Saturday Night Live paid tribute to the late Patrick Stewart following his death at age 86 last week.

The NBC sketch comedy show reaired a brief clip from Stewart’s February 5, 1994, hosting stint, in which he enthusiastically introduced that night’s musical guests, Salt-N-Pepa. Clips of Stewart’s two SNL musical introductions have gone viral in recent years due to his high-energy delivery.

SNL also flashed a title card on screen in Stewart’s memory during Saturday’s show, including one of the bumper promo photos that he shot during the week he hosted in 1994.

The entertainment world has come together over the last few days to share their love and respect for Stewart, following the Thursday, October 29, announcement that he’d died one day earlier following a battle with Alzheimer’s disease and Lewy body dementia.

“SNL” pays tribute to Patrick Stewart. Courtesy NBC

“[Patrick experienced] a brief and poignant journey with Alzheimer’s disease and Lewy body dementia,” his longtime spokesperson, Kelly Bush Novak, confirmed. “He has been held in the warm embrace of family and friends, surrounded by live music, prayer, tears, laughter and the kind of profound emotion that only he could inspire after a lifetime of devotion to us all.”

Novak’s statement concluded, “We will forever cherish the peaceful ease and reflection on his face, an enduring reminder of his invincible grace.”

Stewart’s Alzheimer’s disease and Lewy body dementia diagnoses were not publicly disclosed before his death. (The Mayo Clinic states that Lewy body dementia is the second most common form of Alzheimer’s disease. The condition — which impairs memory and movement as well as causing cognitive decline — develops when protein deposits called Lewy bodies form in the nerve cells of the brain.)

Affirming their commitment to supporting those with the disease, Stewart’s family asked for donations to the Johns Hopkins Alzheimer’s Disease Research Center Fund and Alzheimer’s Foundation of America Caregiver Resources in lieu of sending flowers.

Who Are the Hottest TV and Movie Monsters in Pop Culture History?

📅 Oct 31, 2026  |  Category: Entertainment  |  Read Source →

From Jamie Campbell Bower‘s intriguing take on Stranger Things‘ Vecna to Bill Skarsgård‘s chilling iteration of Nosferatu, there’s a surprising amount of hot monsters on our screens these days.

Skarsgård has made a name for himself playing strangely appealing creatures since his blood-hungry character on Hemlock Grove. From there, Skarsgård terrified Us as Pennywise the Dancing Clown in the It franchise — and as Count Orlok in Nosferatu.

“He’s gross. But it is very sexualized,” he told Esquire in 2024 about the role he considered “erotic” for viewers. “It’s playing with a sexual fetish about the power of the monster and what that appeal has to you.”

Skarsgård continued: “Hopefully you’ll get a little bit attracted by it and disgusted by your attraction at the same time.”

On the small screen, Bower has left many of Us confused by whether we are more attracted to him as Mr. Whatsit or as the sadistic Vecna.

“I’m grateful … the whole effects team — we’re very grateful for the fact that you think [Vecna] is sexy,” Campbell joked in a 2022 Netflix featurette. “If you can’t have me at my Vecna then you don’t deserve me at my One.”
