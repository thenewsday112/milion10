Lions Issue New Statement Regarding Jack Campbell After Scary On-Field Moment

📅 Nov 02, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

The Detroit Lions have issued a new official statement concerning the wellbeing of their star linebacker Jack Campbell amid his hospitalization following a potentially terrifying neck injury.

“Jack Campbell is resting and will remain in a local hospital tonight, for continued evaluation and monitoring,” the team announced in a written statement shared via their official X account on Sunday, November 1. “The team will provide updates as we have them.”

Campbell, 26, was seemingly “immobilized” following what appeared to be a relatively normal tackle against Minnesota Vikings tight end T.J. Hockenson on Sunday during his team’s 19-15 victory over the Vikings.

After the Lions’ athletic training staff swarmed the linebacker — who did not appear to be able to move after his initial fall — and quickly rendered aid, the athlete was carefully moved onto a backboard and carted off the field. An audible hush went over the crowd at Ford Field in Detroit, Michigan, as fans watched on.

Dan Campbell Addresses Jack Campbell's Possible Neck Injury After Awful Scene

Lions head coach Dan Campbell gave a solemn and brief update on the all-pro player’s condition during a press conference shortly after the team’s emotional victory, relaying that the organization would update the press and fans alike as new information regarding the linebacker’s condition became available.

“Let me start with this: You know, thoughts and prayers are with Jack and his family,” Dan, 50, told reporters at the time and before taking questions on the team’s Sunday performance, which handed the Vikings their first 2026-2027 loss.

“He’s being taken care of — we don’t have any new information,” the head coach continued. “We’ll keep you updated as this goes, um, but we’re thinking of him.”

An audibly emotional Erin Andrews reported from the sideline shortly after Jack was carted off the field, sharing what one member of the team said about how important the linebacker is to the organization while seeming fighting back tears.

“He said, ‘This is Jack Campbell’s defense. I don’t hesitate to say that it goes through him and everyone in that locker room knows that,” Andrews, 48, said. “Point blank period.”

Both Vikings and Lions players were visibly emotional in the wake of the incident, with many taking a knee until Jack was carted off the field and others wiping tears from their eyes before retaking the field and resuming play.

“Heavy emotions in Detroit,” Michigan Sportscaster Brad Galli wrote via X after his initial post regarding the incident. “All the Lions players and coaches surrounded Jack Campbell before he was carted off at Ford Field.”

Influencer Allison Kuch Breastfeeds 6-Month-Old Baby During NYC Marathon

📅 Nov 01, 2026  |  Category: Sports  |  Read Source →

Influencer Allison Kuch was able to balance running the 2026 TCS New York City Marathon with motherhood — and without missing a step.

“Six months postpartum, and I’m here,” Kuch, 31, said in a Sunday, November 1, Instagram video, taken at mile marker 17 in the middle of the 26.2-mile course while breastfeeding her 6-month-old daughter, Pepper. “[When] moms put their minds to something and go after it, like, moms are so powerful.”

Kuch received plenty of support in the comments.

“You are incredible! Mile 17 is where everything starts to break down and here you are shining, inspiring, and breastfeeding!” Tyler Cameron, who has run the NYC Marathon multiple times in past years, replied. “Unreal! Honored to call you a friend.”

Allison Kuch Says Postpartum Experience Is ‘So Different’ With Baby No. 2

Brylee Armstrong added, “My idol [for real].”

Kuch gave birth to Pepper in April. (She also shares daughter Scottie, 2, with husband Isaac Rochell.)

Rochell, 31, and Allison’s sister, Emily Kuch, were among the influencer’s biggest cheerleaders on race day.

“WTF is nonchalant,” the former NFL player wrote via Instagram on Sunday, showing off his Allison-inspired purple T-shirt, multiple cameras and a giant poster with his wife’s face on it. “So locked #nycmarathon.”

Courtesy of Allison Kuch/Instagram

Allison revealed earlier this week that she would be running the marathon with makeup brand Maybelline “for mental health” initiatives.

“Fun fact — there are ‘lactation stations’ along the route, so you don’t have to pack a [breast] pump,” she wrote via her Instagram Stories on Friday, October 30, revealing her mid-race nursing plan. “They have pumps provided at the start line and there’s three stops on the route.”

For Allison, she wanted to run the marathon to teach her daughters an impactful life lesson.

How Allison Kuch's Husband Is Helping During Post-Baby 'Identity Crisis'

“I can’t give up on my dreams, my daughters are watching,” she added via Instagram on Friday.

Before making her way to the starting line, Allison has been candid about her postpartum experience since welcoming Pepper.

“When people ask, ‘How are you doing postpartum?’ My brain goes to two places: sleep and mental health,” she said on the latest episode of the couple’s “Couldn’t Agree Less” Netflix podcast. “Here’s my issue with postpartum: Everyone worries about the baby, about birth [in the moment]. … Four months postpartum, nobody’s checking in, and I would say that’s the hardest time of postpartum.”

Allison added at the time, “Four months postpartum is hard because you’re out of newborn-ness, your baby’s more aware, they don’t want to be set down, you’re going through the four-month sleep regression, you’re still breast-feeding. People are expecting you to be back and, like, mama is not back.”
